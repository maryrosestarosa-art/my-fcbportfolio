About

As a Population Program Officer, I help communities and institutions integrate population concerns into development planning and public service delivery. My work involves population and development planning, demographic data analysis, program coordination, monitoring and evaluation, and capacity development. I am particularly interested in adolescent health, responsible parenthood and family planning, and research that informs local policies and programs. Through collaboration with local governments and partner organizations, I help translate population data into practical actions that respond to community needs and support informed decisions.
